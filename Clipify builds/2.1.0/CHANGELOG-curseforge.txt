Clipify 2.1.0

Added
- Minecraft 26.3 support (OpenGL and Vulkan). Clipify now ships for 1.21.11, 26.1.2 and 26.3.
- Volume control in the clip editor, next to the fullscreen button: drag 0-200%, scroll to step, click the speaker to mute. The preview plays at that level and saving writes it into the file (muted = no sound track).

Changed
- Clipify now asks before downloading FFmpeg: a one-time screen per instance shows the size and source, and nothing downloads until you click Download.
- New mod icon, matching the CurseForge page.

Fixed
- Clips turning into a slideshow on slow PCs. Missed frames no longer crowd out real ones, so clips stay real-time and in sync with their audio.
