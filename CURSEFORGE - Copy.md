**Does Clipify need a separate app or account?**<br>
No. Clipify runs inside Minecraft - no separate desktop app and no account required.

**How do I save a clip?**<br>
Set a clip hotkey in **Clipify Settings -> Hotkeys**. Each clip hotkey can save a different replay length, from 5 seconds to 10 minutes.


**Where are clips saved?**<br>
Clips: `.minecraft/clipify/clips/`<br>
Screenshots: `.minecraft/clipify/screenshots/`


**Do I need it installed on the server?**<br>
No. Clipify is client-side only, so it works on any server.


**Does it record audio?**<br>
Yes. Microphone audio works on Windows, macOS, and Linux. PC/desktop audio is currently Windows-only.
